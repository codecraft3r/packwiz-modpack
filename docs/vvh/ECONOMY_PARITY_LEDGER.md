## Market schedule

| Service group | Cooldown | Scope | Currency |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Building services | 180 seconds | one shared entitlement per FTB Team payment | Sprockets or Bevels |
| Progression and civic services | 300 seconds | one shared entitlement per FTB Team payment | Sprockets, Bevels, or a Cog |
| Rumour Ledger | 604800 seconds | team-scoped faucet | 1 Bevel-equivalent |

The faucet cannot self-fund a premium service or the complete 53
Bevel-equivalent board: one team needs 53 weekly claims from the faucet alone.
At fixed population two, one two-player team has 184 one-time
Bevel-equivalents and one Bevel-equivalent faucet per week; two one-player
teams have 200 aggregate one-time currency and two per week. Currency items
can transfer or trade, so this compares issuance and claim scope without a
hard economic pooling barrier. The optional larger scenario of two
two-player teams has 368 aggregate one-time currency and two per week. These
are source models, not live claim proof.

Paid stock is intentionally finite common-material help. It is not an infinite
exploit merely because a later task can use some of it. Review each service for
recycling, NPC exchange, denomination conversion, task shortcuts, and one
payment → one shared entitlement behavior. A repeatable service that returns
its input or supplies a decisive progression workstation remains prohibited.

Concord Bond currently costs 2 Sprockets (4 Bevel-equivalents) and supplies
24.5 authored 64-stack equivalents of shaped civic stock. Works Kit costs one
Sprocket (2 Bevel-equivalents) and supplies 14.375; two Works orders would
therefore supply 28.75 at Concord's price. Concord is 85.2% of Works by this
coarse volume metric and is acceptable only as a curated roads/bridges
convenience bundle. A one-Cog price would drop it to about 43% of equal-price
Works stock and should be rebalanced or explicitly approved as a premium.

overflow, late joiners, and cooldown enforcement require a live FTB Quests
test. Static totals describe authored rewards and declared economy; they do not

matrix.
