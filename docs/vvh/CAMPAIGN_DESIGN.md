# VvH Concord Campaign

This is the authoring contract for the live eight-chapter campaign generated by
`scripts/vvh_campaign_v3.py`. The generated SNBT is authoritative at runtime;
this document explains the intended player experience and the constraints that
future edits must preserve.

## Player promise

The campaign is written for a persistent world with three to four regular
players and occasional groups of up to eight. It begins at the observed server
state: diamond tools, no full diamond armor, and early Create power with one
working windmill. Fast grinders may reach optional mastery sooner, but they
cannot turn that lead into a repeatable power or currency engine.

The campaign has four connected rhythms:

1. A closed-loop charter establishes claims, consent, and FTB Team scope.
2. Three equally visible callings provide distinct mechanical identities.
3. A short central progression leads to specialties and a shared charter.
4. Public works, one-session events, and priced services create reasons to meet
   again after individual progression diverges.

## Chapter architecture

| Chapter | Purpose | Mechanical identity |
|---|---|---|
| 01 · Island Charter | Closed-loop onboarding | claims, rivalry consent, team scope |
| 02 · Three Callings | Symmetric first identity | House, Commons, or Order |
| 03 · Lantern Order | Hunter route | Vampirism Hunter tools, Holy support, watchposts, field records |
| 04 · House of Night | Vampire route | blood infrastructure, Blood utility magic, hospitality, night routes |
| 05 · Free Companies | Civic route | early Create, visible trade, mapping, village services, roads |
| 06 · Common Ground | Whole-server worldbuilding | simple central spine plus archive, watch, and market contributions |
| 07 · Odd Hours | Linked one-session events | repairs, magic exchange, co-crafting, photography, courier work, auction |
| 08 · Market Services | Currency sinks and fallback | weekly team-scoped kits priced by denomination |

## Graph grammar

- Chapter 01 has exactly one root and one terminal.
- Chapter 02 shows all three callings and accepts any one.
- Chapters 03–05 use the same visual language: three accessible core steps,
  specialties fanning outward, one breadth gate, and a team charter. Enhanced
  Hunter/Vampire equipment and the Mechanical Arm are optional mastery nodes.
- Chapter 06 keeps group worldbuilding on a straight central spine. The archive,
  emergency watch, and market are side contributions; the capstone requires the
  shared roof plus any two.
- Chapter 07 shows all six events on one row and accepts any three.
- Chapter 08 is a service board, not a progression ladder.

## Writing contract

Every quest separates lore from what the task UI verifies. Lore names specific
people, habits, objects, and consequences without repeating the objective.
Player-facing copy contains no balance claims, implementation notes, or promises
that an inventory task proved a finished build.

Quest titles remain four words or fewer. Item and advancement tasks provide hard
criteria. Checkmarks are reserved for rules, breadth acknowledgements, spell
demonstrations, public usability that Minecraft cannot inspect, hosted events,
and the weekly written civic report.

## Progression and cooperation

- The existing windmill is acknowledged; the Free Company core adds a simple
  second power source and public processing line.
- Advanced Vampirism and Create components do not gate the week-one charter.
- Normal faction equipment rewards are substantial catch-up support, not diamond
  armor or a permanent tier skip.
- Public projects combine material proof with a teammate usability attestation.
- Spell schools are thematically aligned, never synchronized to live faction
  state through KubeJS.
- No custom KubeJS quest logic is used.

## Source and asset policy

Every non-vanilla item, advancement, spell, and namespace must resolve through
the current Packwiz index and `docs/vvh/id_catalog.json`. Cobblemon is absent and
must not appear. Artwork must resolve from the shipped resource pack; source
review boards are evidence of graph composition only, not in-client screenshots.

Regenerate and check with:

```powershell
python -X utf8 scripts/vvh_campaign_v3.py
python -X utf8 scripts/vvh_campaign_v3.py --check
python -X utf8 scripts/vvh_sync_manifest.py . --check
python -X utf8 scripts/vvh_sync_catalog.py . --check
```
