# VvH Concord Quest Map

The live campaign contains 62 quests across eight chapters.

## 01 · Island Charter

`Island Charter` opens three required promises—`Claim Clearly`, `Rivalry With
Consent`, and `Choose Teammates`—which converge on `Sign the Charter`.

## 02 · Three Callings

`Three Callings` fans symmetrically to `Join the House`, `Join the Commons`, and
`Join the Order`. Completing any one opens `Keep Doors Open`; the other callings
remain visible and may be pursued later.

## 03 · Lantern Order

`Salt and Steel` → `Consecrated Work` → `Long Watch` forms the accessible core.
`Mercy Manual`, `Stone Lantern`, and `Field Ledger` form the specialty fan.
`Bright Oath` is optional enhanced mastery. Core plus any two specialties opens
`Two Lanterns`, then the team-scoped `Lantern Charter`.

## 04 · House of Night

`First Thirst` → `Red Measure` → `Inherited Edge` forms the accessible core.
`Scarlet Script`, `House of Doors`, and `Moon Courier` form the specialty fan.
`Night's Due` is optional enhanced mastery. Core plus any two specialties opens
`Two Seals`, then the team-scoped `Night Charter`.

## 05 · Free Companies

`Open Ledger` → `Second Current` → `Public Line` turns existing early Create
progress into a shared service. `Map Room`, `Village Hall`, `Market Clock`, and
`Road Keeper` form the specialty fan. `Common Engine` is optional Mechanical Arm
mastery. Core plus any two specialties opens `Three Hands`, then `Free Charter`.

## 06 · Common Ground

The group worldbuilding lane runs straight through `Town Bell`, `Known Roads`,
`Common Forge`, and `Shared Roof`. `Public Archive`, `Night Watch`, and `Market
Day` are side contributions. `First Concord` requires the roof plus any two side
contributions and pays a team Cog with public project stock.

## 07 · Odd Hours

Six one-session events sit on one row: `Blackout Drill`, `The Red Table`, `Four
Hands`, `Photo Hunt`, `Courier Relay`, and `Open Auction`. Any three open the
team-scoped `Common Cause` capstone.

## 08 · Market Services

`Read the Board` opens five weekly currency sinks: `Field Kit`, `Works Kit`,
`Arcane Kit`, `Foundry Kit`, and `Concord Bond`. `Know the Coins` explains exact
denominations. After `Common Cause`, `Rumour Ledger` provides the single slow
fallback faucet: one team Bevel per week.
