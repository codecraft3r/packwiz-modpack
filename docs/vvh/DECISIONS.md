# VvH Season One Decisions

## Factions and magic

- House of Night points toward Blood magic and controlled ritual utility.
- Lantern Order points toward Holy magic, refuge, healing, and public safety.
- Free Companies mediate between both schools with limited neutral utility.
- Iron's Spells schools remain open to every player; faction identity is thematic rather than a hard lock.
- Vampirism state and FTB Teams progress remain separate, with peer/host confirmation for shared faction milestones.

## Quest shape and balance

- Chapters 2 and 3 expose distinct progression and world-building lanes.
- Chapter 4 provides contracts, courier work, neutral workshops, mediation, and shared magical services.
- Checkmarks cover reviewed builds and social services; no custom KubeJS quest logic is added.
- Rewards provide time-saving materials, construction support, consumables, and capped player choice.
- No quest grants permanent levels, major armor tiers, boss gear, high-tier spell power, or a new currency source.
- Weekly Bevel exchanges consume existing currency and cannot mint Bevels or reproduce their own input.

## Art

- All chapter panoramas and the Blood/Holy/mediator support set use the Poiesis namespace and documented dimensions.
- Transparent crests and the rune sheet use alpha PNGs with nearest-neighbor scaling and clean corners.
